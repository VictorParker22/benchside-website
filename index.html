Your page

One link does the selling.

Your page lists your passes, what each one includes and the price. Athletes pay by card in any browser, with no download first, and their plan is waiting when they open the app.

  • Monthly or yearly passes, or a season with a set number of payments
  • Card checkout by Stripe, on the web or in the app
  • Nobody joins your roster without buying, asking or accepting your link
Inbox

Monday, handled.

Check-ins and messages land in one inbox, sorted by who needs you. Today shows who owes a reply, who’s slipping and who just hit a PR. If it isn’t something to do, it isn’t on the screen.

  • Weekly check-ins: effort, anything that hurt, the session that stood out
  • Photos and videos in one thread per athlete
  • Drafts waiting for you are marked
Coach Elite

Your assistant drafts. You send.

It reads the athlete’s check-in and their logged sets and drafts a reply in your voice. Nothing reaches an athlete until you approve it, and every athlete is told you use one.

  • You edit, approve and send every message
  • Turn it on or off whenever you like
  • Included with Coach Elite and Founding

Questions

Questions coaches ask.

More in Help
Do my athletes need the app to pay?

No. They pay on your page in any browser, then get the app to train. Their plan is waiting when they sign in with the same email.

Does Benchside work on Android?

Benchside is an iPhone app today. There is no Android app yet, so an athlete needs an iPhone to train with you. Buying a pass works in any browser.

How do payouts work?

Payments run on Stripe. You connect a bank once, inside the app, and Stripe sends your payouts to it. Athletes can’t buy a pass until Stripe says your account can take charges.

Does the AI message my athletes?

Never on its own. With Coach Elite it drafts a reply from the check-in and the logged sets; you edit, approve and send.

Can I bring athletes I already coach?

Yes. Send them your link. They buy a pass on your page and their plan is ready in the app.
