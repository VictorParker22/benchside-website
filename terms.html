In plain words

Benchside is where independent coaches sell training passes and run their athletes’ weeks. The coach sells the pass; Benchside runs the platform and processes the payment through Stripe. An open pass renews until you cancel it; a season is billed the number of payments shown before you pay, then stops by itself. Refunds are the coach’s call and are issued through the app. Switching coaches or deleting your account ends your pass that day, and time already paid isn’t refunded, so ask your coach before you switch. Coach Elite is a subscription for coaches, bought on the web through Stripe (or, if you bought it there, through Apple under Apple’s terms). Training carries risk and nothing on Benchside is medical advice. You must be 16 or older. An account starts as a coach or an athlete; a coach can also train with another coach.

Benchside was called FitLink until October 2026. It is the same service under a new name; if you agreed to the FitLink terms or privacy policy, these replace them.

4. Passes and subscriptions (athletes)

  • What a pass is. A pass is what the coach describes on it: an open pass, billed every month or every year until you cancel it, or a season of a fixed number of weeks. Every pass charges the price shown per month, or per year for a yearly price. A season charges it for the number of payments shown before you pay (for example, two monthly payments for an 8-week season) and then stops billing; that number is fixed when you buy, and a later change to the season by the coach does not change it. The price shown at checkout is the price you pay, plus any tax that applies where you live.
  • Buying a pass joins the coach. When you buy a pass you join that coach’s roster, and the coach can see your training data, logged sets, check-ins and messages for as long as you are on it. If you were training with a different coach, buying a pass from a new one moves you to the new coach; your history stays in the previous coach’s records, and your pass with them ends as described in section 3 (no refund of time already paid).
  • Renewal. An open pass renews automatically at the end of each period, at the price shown, until you cancel it. A season renews only until its last payment: nothing more is charged after it, and the season ends when the period that payment covered ends. You then keep your copy of the season and everything you logged, and you stay on the coach’s roster; to keep training on the coach’s plan, buy another pass.
  • Cancelling. You can cancel a pass from the app at any time. Cancelling stops the next payment; you keep access to the coach’s plan until the end of the period you have already paid for. After that, access to the plan is no longer part of your pass and may be closed, including for a season cancelled before its last payment. Your logged sets, check-ins and messages stay, and you stay on the coach’s roster. A price change never applies to a period you have already paid for.
  • Failed payments. If a renewal payment fails, the pass goes on hold until payment succeeds. Your coach can see that it is on hold.
  • A coach may end a pass. A coach may stop selling a pass or remove you from their roster. If that happens before the end of a period you have paid for, see refunds below.

5. Payments, fees, refunds and disputes

Refunds

  • Refunds are at the coach’s discretion and are issued through the app, within 90 days of the payment. If you want a refund, ask your coach. The coach refunds the full payment or half of it, and either ends the pass that day or lets it run to the end of the period you paid for. The notice you receive says which.
  • Benchside may also refund a payment where the law, Stripe’s rules or the card network’s rules require it, or where a coach’s account has been closed before a paid period was delivered.
  • Switching coaches or deleting your account is not a refund. Either one ends your pass that day; time already paid is not refunded, and any unpaid amount on the pass is forgiven. If you want money back, ask your coach before you switch or delete.

  • If something is wrong with a payment, contact your coach or us first; most problems are quicker to fix that way than through your bank.

While a dispute is open, the disputed amount is withheld from the coach and may be recovered from the coach’s payouts; if the dispute is resolved in the coach’s favour the amount is paid to them again.
