Questions

Pricing questions.

More in Help
What is the fee charged on?

Only on passes athletes buy through Benchside. Sell nothing, pay nothing.

Are card processing fees included?

Yes. A pass is charged through Benchside’s Stripe account and the athlete’s payment, minus the Benchside fee, is transferred to yours. Stripe’s card processing (about 2.9% + 30¢ a payment) comes out of Benchside’s fee, not out of what you keep. When you refund a payment, Stripe may keep its processing fee.

Do my athletes pay anything extra?

On the standard plans, athletes pay exactly the price you set: there is no processing charge or surcharge at checkout. A coach who uses a booking fee ($12 a payment) has it included in the total the athlete is shown before paying, never added after.

Is there an athlete limit?

On the free plan, your sixth paying athlete’s payment goes through and they join. From then on you need Elite to keep changing your athletes’ plans; their payments keep coming either way. Coach Elite and Founding have no cap.

Is there a minimum price?

Yes: a pass costs at least $25 a payment.

How do I start or stop Elite?

Subscribe on the web from your coach dashboard; the app opens your dashboard. You pay on Stripe’s page, monthly ($29.99) or yearly ($249). Manage or cancel it from the link in your Stripe receipt; Elite stays on until the end of the period you paid for. If you already have Elite through the App Store, it stays managed in the App Store.

How does the lower fee reach my existing passes?

The fee is set on the server from your plan and applied to every pass payment, including renewals of passes your athletes already hold.

What does a dispute cost?

$15 for each dispute, won or lost; an inquiry costs nothing. It comes out of what Stripe pays you for that payment or for your next ones.

What is Founding?

15 coaches get Coach Elite free for a year and a 5% fee. The free year starts with the first payment one of your athletes makes after your spot is granted. After it, Elite is $29.99 a month if you keep it, or you move to the free plan. Applications close Dec 31. Apply for a Founding spot.
